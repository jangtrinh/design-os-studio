## 1. Summary and user story

As a designer running coding agents inside Studio, I want the Agents panel, its terminal and every agent card to look like the rest of the Untitled-styled app, so the agent area no longer reads as a separate product with its own greys, blue chips and white-on-orange buttons.

The first job is the restyle: colours move to the token contract's chrome constants; buttons, tabs, chips, badges, popovers and dialogs take spec C's widgets; sizes follow R5 (editor panel: text-xs rows and labels, button xs 32, radius-md 8). What the terminal prints (ANSI colours, cell font, cursor, cell selection) is content and stays as it is (controller default).

The second job is the simplification the owner ruled on 2026-10-29 (R19–R24; audit `reviews/I-chat-panel-simplify-audit.md`; design reference `evidence/i-chat-simplify/after-v2.png`, generator `chat_panel_mock_v2.py`; `after.png` stays the reference for the attach-menu state only, because `after-v2.png` drops it). The audit measured the clutter. Widgets are counted in L's committed layout JSON at baseline `dd53ba7a`: the sum of `count` over the `named` entries whose `region` is `left_panel`, plus the `unnamed` entries whose `region` is `left_panel` (scroll internals are in neither list; `named` also holds recorded rects that are not interactive, such as `agents-composer-box` and `left-panel-resize-handle`, and the audit's numbers include them): **33** in `i-agents` (a new session), **38** with Skills open (`agents-skills`), and **26** on approval per the audit (L's three approval cases count 22, 22 and 24 with the same definition: `i-agents-approval-command`, `agents-approval-queued`, `agents-approval-open-tab`); **two header rows** (the 36 bar and the 24 context strip); and a **status line under the composer**. After: one header row; an empty new-chat body with the agent picker as a pill in the composer toolbar (R24); a toolbar of `+`, agent pill, `Ask first ▾` and Send; no starter suggestions (R20); an approval card and a Skills popover that show only what the user decides on. R23: v0.7.5 changes the chrome only. The chat body stays the CLI's terminal (PTY): Studio does not draw what the agent prints, and a rendered-messages chat is designed separately. R1 (layout unchanged) does not bind this panel (R19); the layout gate takes its new geometry through the intended-change process of section 12.

## 8. Edge cases

1. **Many tabs (10 or more).** Tabs shrink to their `56` floor and the strip stops at the first tab that does not fit (`:5795-5801`); the rest are reached from the `⋯` sessions list. `+`, `⋯` and Stop keep their widths, so the overflow point is the right reserve of rule 13. Check with 10 tabs at 400 wide and at 260: no tab overlaps Stop, `+` or `⋯`. A hidden tab that needs the user puts the dot on `⋯`.
2. **Narrow panel** (the Agents panel's minimum width, 260: `shell.rs:1606`, `ai_panel_width.clamp(260.0, …)`). Header: at least one tab, `+` and `⋯`; Stop, while shown, takes 64 and the strip loses a tab, never `+` or `⋯`. Composer toolbar: `+` and Send never shrink; the agent pill's label elides first (floor 72 including the chevron), then `Ask first ▾` becomes icon-only (`Icon::Lock` for Ask, `Icon::Unlock` for Skip, 32×32, label in its tooltip and menu); the row then needs `32 + 72 + 32 + 32` plus gaps, which fits the 236 of inner width. Chips wrap. Menus clamp to the panel (`clamp(panel − 16, 196, 232)`). No control is narrower than 24.
3. **Approval queue with long tool input.** The command row elides at one line (32 high); `1 of N` stays right-aligned in the title row and the title elides before it; the card is 136 outer (Claude) or 96 (others), so the viewport's top content shifts down by that when a card is shown (the card overlays the viewport top, `viewport_rect` inset, not a layout region). N counts every tab.
4. **Inverse video** (`:6734-6737`) uses `TERM_BG_VIEWPORT` as glyph colour: with the alias it becomes `#0A0A0A` on `#E4E4E7`, contrast 15.60:1 (was 15.18:1). No change in meaning.
5. **Legacy chat branch** must be restyled even though new tabs default to PTY; reach it with `--panel agents-chat` (`I-03`); from the panel it is reached through `⋯` then `Classic chat` (rule 13), which replaces the composer's book button.
6. **No skills attached**: no skills chip is drawn, and `Skills…` in the `+` menu shows no `N on` caption.
7. **Disabled Approve** (decision in flight): 50 % opacity on fill and label, still `TEXT_ON_BRAND`.
8. **Shell and DESIGN:OS help tabs** carry no status dot unless they fail. The composer is disabled with `Select an agent tab` as its placeholder, once (the caption under the box that repeated it is gone).
9. **Light-on-dark labels on presence colours** (`AGENT_LABEL_TEXT`) change to the dark label `#0A0A0A` by the contract; the four pairs are C-1.41–C-1.44 (10.12, 6.82, 5.88 and 9.24:1).
10. **Approval on another tab** (#1195): the card shows `Open tab` for the tab that waits, at the same 96 outer height as the other non-Claude cards; with several pending approvals the "1 of N" count stays right-aligned. A Claude approval on the active tab keeps the 136 outer height. While the New chat state covers a live Claude tab that waits, its card is `Open tab` too (rule 14).
11. **Gemini tab**: a Gemini CLI tab has no Stop control (no interrupt row) and its approval card is the non-Claude card (Show prompt or Open tab); the tab status "Needs approval" uses the same warning look as the other CLIs.
12. **Image-job states** (queued, working, failed): the chip uses `Loading/Inline` for queued and working, `TEXT_ERROR` for failed; the Expand and Erase popovers keep their sizes except buttons to `CONTROL_XS`.
13. **No agents installed.** The body is empty; the pill reads `Choose agent`; the placeholder reads `No agent CLI found. Open the agent menu to install one.`; every built-in row is disabled with `Install`; `More…` still offers `Other CLI…` and `Shell`; Send is disabled with the tooltip `Install an agent first`, and the draft is kept.
14. **Every agent signed out.** Only Claude can be known signed out (rule 15): its row shows `Sign in`. The other rows show nothing, and nothing says `unknown`. While Codex, Grok, Antigravity and Gemini CLI cannot report a sign-in state, a signed-out one is found when the agent starts and shows the start-failure card with `Run login` (rule 20).
15. **Selection of 0 or of many layers.** 0: no chip. 1: `Hero`. 2: `Hero, Title`. More: `Hero, Title, +N`. No name known: `N layers`. A long name elides to the chip's width (148 at most, 72 at least); chips wrap when the box is narrow.
16. **A long session name in a tab.** The title elides with `…` to the tab's label width (`widgets::elide`); the tooltip carries the full title and the status; the dot comes first and never elides.
17. **A refused send keeps its draft.** The refusal shows as the caption inside the box (rule 16 h) and the placeholder stays hidden under the draft; the caption clears as today.
18. **The sessions list is empty** (no saved session): `⋯` still opens the popover with its `No sessions yet` row (today's empty row) and the footer items.
