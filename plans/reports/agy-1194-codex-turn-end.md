## 4. Per-CLI Scope (`#1194` AC-4)

| Mechanism | Presets in Scope | Presets Excluded & Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1.5 s PTY-heuristic `Working(_) -> Done` decay** (`resolve_pty_heuristic_status`, `sync_pty_heuristic_status_decay`, `AgentCliPreset::uses_pty_heuristic`) | **`Codex` only** (`AgentCliPreset::Codex`) | **`Claude`, `Grok`, `Antigravity`, `Gemini`** are driven by structured hook events in `agent-events.jsonl` (`Stop` -> `Done`, `PermissionRequest`/`Notification` -> `WaitingForInput`) and must not decay mid-turn when a quiet tool runs.<br>**`OpenCode`, `Shell`, `DesignOsHelp`** do not use the Codex PTY heuristic. |
| **PTY process-exit status mapping** (`TabTurnTracker::on_process_exit` + `ProcessExit::from_pty_status`) | **All agent CLI presets** (`Claude`, `Codex`, `Grok`, `Antigravity`, `Gemini`, `OpenCode`) when the tab is `Working` or `WaitingForInput` (or `turn_tracker.in_turn`) at process exit | **`Shell`, `DesignOsHelp`** (`tab.is_shell_process()` returns early in the exit poll loop). Tabs that are already `Idle`, `Done`, `Stopped`, or `Error` with `in_turn == false` keep their status (`on_process_exit` returns `None`). |
