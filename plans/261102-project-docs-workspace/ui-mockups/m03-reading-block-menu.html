Customers can pay by card, Apple Pay or bank transfer. Methods appear in the order below.

  • Card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
  • Apple Pay on Safari and iOS
  • Bank transfer for orders over $200
Card payments use 3-D Secure when the issuer asks for it.
