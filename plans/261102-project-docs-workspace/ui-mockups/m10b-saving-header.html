Payment methods

How customers pay at checkout, and what happens when a payment fails.

Accepted methods

Customers can pay by card, Apple Pay or bank transfer. Methods appear in the order below. Bank transfer is held for review

  • Card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
  • Apple Pay on Safari and iOS
  • Bank transfer for orders over $200
Card payments use 3-D Secure when the issuer asks for it.
